记$f(x) = X @ weight + bias$，$y$为预测值，用{-1, 1}作为正负类标签的时候，$p(y|x;w,b) = \sigma(y \cdot f(x))$, 故交叉熵损失函数为 $ L = -\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}log(\sigma(y \cdot f(x)))$ (最大化对数似然)

接下来算各个参数的解析梯度，注意有重要结论$\sigma’(x) = \sigma(x) \cdot (1 - \sigma(x)) $

  • $grad_\hat{y}$：我们记$a_i = -Y_i \cdot \hat{y_i}$，则$ L = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}log(1 + exp(a_i))$
    故有
    $$
    \frac{dL}{d\hat{y_i}} = \frac{dL}{da_i} \cdot \frac{da_i}{d\hat{y_i}} = -\frac{1}{n}\sigma(a_i) \cdot Y_i
    $$
